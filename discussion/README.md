# Community Discussion: Shared Standards for Transparent and Reproducible TEA

The workshop closes with an open discussion on how the TEA community can move toward shared, open standards, in
the way the LCA community did with tools such as openLCA and Brightway. This folder collects the agenda, the
notes taken during the session, and the outcomes agreed on.

## Agenda (draft)

1. Where do TEA studies diverge today? Cost basis and year, location factors, accuracy classes, financing
   assumptions, treatment of uncertainty, reporting of results.
2. What would a minimum reporting standard for a TEA look like, and what should a reference input file contain?
3. Shared data: cost correlations, CEPCI and location factors, utility prices. How do we collect, review, and
   version them in the open?
4. Tooling: what should OpenPyTEA and comparable tools provide to make studies comparable, and how do we keep
   them interoperable with process simulators and LCA software?
5. Next steps: a community forum, a shared repository, and who volunteers for what.

## Files

- `notes.md`: notes taken during the discussion (added after the workshop)
- `outcomes.md`: agreed actions, volunteers, and follow-up dates (added after the workshop)
